INT8 卷积测试向量清单
hex 约定: _w=1值/字(符号扩展)  _b=4个int8打包/字(小端)
======================================================================
t1_tiny                x(4, 4, 1) w(3, 3, 1, 1) -> y(2, 2, 1) stride=1 pad=0 shift=0 relu=1
                       最小用例 4x4x1 k3 -> 2x2x1，先让数据通路跑起来
t2_stride2             x(5, 5, 1) w(3, 3, 1, 1) -> y(2, 2, 1) stride=2 pad=0 shift=0 relu=1
                       stride=2 跨步窗口，5x5 -> 2x2
t3_saturate            x(4, 4, 1) w(3, 3, 1, 1) -> y(2, 2, 1) stride=1 pad=0 shift=0 relu=1
                       全 127 输入+权重 -> 累加 145161 -> 必须饱和到 127
t4_multi               x(5, 5, 2) w(3, 3, 2, 2) -> y(3, 3, 2) stride=1 pad=0 shift=0 relu=1
                       C=2 OC=2，5x5 -> 3x3x2，测多通道累加
t5_requant             x(6, 6, 2) w(3, 3, 2, 2) -> y(4, 4, 2) stride=1 pad=0 shift=4 relu=1
                       shift=4 算术右移 + ReLU，测量化与负数右移
t6_edge                x(4, 4, 1) w(3, 3, 1, 1) -> y(2, 2, 1) stride=1 pad=0 shift=0 relu=0
                       边界：0/±极值/交替权重，且关掉 ReLU 看负数饱和
t7_mnist28_pad1        x(28, 28, 1) w(3, 3, 1, 4) -> y(28, 28, 4) stride=1 pad=1 shift=0 relu=1
                       28x28x1 pad1 k3 OC=4 -> 28x28x4，给之后 MNIST demo 用（文件较大）
